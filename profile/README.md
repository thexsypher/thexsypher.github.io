### 🏛️ Production Systems Architecture

Our publication platform is built from first principles for uncompromising performance, strict zero-trust security, and instant global distribution:

* **Frontend & SSR:** [Next.js 16](https://nextjs.org/) App Router with Turbopack, standalone ARM64 multi-stage containers, and Tailwind CSS.
* **Data & Relational Tier:** [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/) with [Drizzle ORM](https://orm.drizzle.team/) on Neon Serverless, fronted by connection pooling (PgBouncer).
* **Decoupled Object Storage:** [Cloudflare R2](https://www.cloudflare.com/developer-platform/r2/) S3 storage for article content bodies, TipTap AST trees, and high-resolution assets.
* **Asynchronous Jobs:** [BullMQ](https://bullmq.io/) and Redis AOF persistence for scheduled article broadcasting, newsletter delivery, and analytics flushing.
* **Identity & Governance:** NextAuth v5 with capability-based RBAC, TOTP 2FA encryption, and granular permission scoping.
